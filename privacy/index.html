Pixqo for Android

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

This policy explains how the Pixqo Android app (package com.subirhazra.pixqo) handles information. Pixqo is a free AI image generator made by Subir Hazra (HazraX Labs). It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

In short
  • Your images and prompt history stay on your phone.
  • To create an image, your prompt is sent to Pollinations.ai, the image service Pixqo uses.
  • AI Upscale runs entirely on your phone. Your image is never uploaded.
  • If you report an image, the report is sent to us through Google Forms so we can review it.
  • We don't sell data, show ads, or track you.

1. Information stored on your device

Pixqo keeps the following in the app's private storage on your phone. It is not uploaded to us:

  • Images you generate, your likes, and upscaled copies
  • Your recent prompts (up to 10)
  • Your generation settings and preferences

You can delete these in Settings → Clear library and Clear prompt history, or by uninstalling the app.

2. Creating images (Pollinations.ai)

When you tap Generate, the app sends your prompt, any "what to avoid" text, and the image settings (style, size and seed) directly from your phone to Pollinations.ai, a third-party image service. Like any website, Pollinations also receives your device's IP address when your phone connects to it. Pollinations handles this under its own terms and privacy policy. Please don't put personal or sensitive information in your prompts.

Pixqo asks Pollinations to use its strict safety mode, and also blocks prompts for sexual content or content that sexualizes minors before they are sent.

3. AI Upscale (optional)

Upscaling only happens when you tap Upscale 4x with AI. It runs entirely on your phone, using an AI model built into the app. The image is not uploaded anywhere, and the upscaled copy is saved only in the app's private storage on your phone. If the image you upscale is still online (not yet saved), the app first downloads it from the image service, just as it does when showing it.

4. Reporting an image

Any image can be reported from inside the app, without leaving it. When you send a report, the app submits it to our Google Form (a Google service; responses go to a private spreadsheet only we can see). A report contains:

  • the reason you chose and any note you write,
  • the prompt, "what to avoid" text, style engine, seed and dimensions used to make the image,
  • the image's web link, if it has one, and
  • the app version and platform.

Reports do not include the image file and aren't linked to your identity. Like any website, Google receives your device's IP address when the report is sent, and handles it under its own privacy policy; we don't see it. We use reports only to review problems and improve Pixqo's safety filtering, and delete them after at most 12 months.

5. App permissions

  • Internet: to create images and send reports.
  • Saving to your gallery: when you tap Save, Pixqo adds that image to Pictures/Pixqo. Pixqo doesn't read your existing photos. On Android 9 and older this uses the storage permission.
  • Vibration: for haptic feedback.

6. Sharing and selling

We don't sell your information or share it with advertisers. Data is only sent to Pollinations.ai to create images, and to Google Forms when you report an image, as described above. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

7. Security

All connections the app makes use HTTPS encryption. Data stored on your phone is protected by Android's app sandbox.

8. Children

Pixqo is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
