Community Rules

Effective date: October 28, 2026

Pixqo's Explore tab is a public gallery of AI upscales made by people using the app. These rules apply to everything shared there. You agree to them before your first shared upscale.

In short
  • Free upscales are shared in Explore with their prompt and settings, without your name.
  • Anyone using Pixqo can see, like, save and share images in Explore.
  • No sexual content, nothing involving minors, no gore, hate, harassment or illegal content.
  • You can remove your own shared images at any time.
  • Reported images are taken down, and installs that break the rules are blocked.

1. What is shared

When you upscale an image on the free tier, the upscaled image is published in Explore together with its prompt, any "what to avoid" text, the style engine, seed and size. No name, account, email or device information is shown. Pixqo has no accounts; your posts are linked only to a random ID created when the app was installed, which lets you like a post once and remove your own posts.

2. What is not allowed

Don't create or share images or prompts that:

  • are sexual or sexually suggestive, or contain nudity;
  • involve, sexualize or endanger minors in any way;
  • show graphic violence, gore, self-harm or cruelty to animals;
  • promote hatred of, or harass, any person or group;
  • depict real people in a misleading, sexual or demeaning way, or include someone's private information;
  • promote terrorism, illegal drugs, weapons trafficking or other illegal activity;
  • are spam or attempt to abuse or overload the service.

Pixqo blocks many such prompts before an image is made, and the image service runs in its strict safety mode, but you are responsible for what you choose to upscale and share.

3. Reporting and removal

Every image in Explore has a Report button. Reports go to the developer, and an image reported by several people is hidden automatically until it is reviewed. Images that break these rules are removed, and the app install that shared them can be blocked from sharing and upscaling. We review reports as quickly as we can.

4. Removing your own images

Open the image in your Library and tap Remove next to "Shared in Explore", or open it in Explore and tap Remove. It disappears from Explore immediately. Deleting it from your library also offers to remove it from Explore. Copies other people saved before removal are outside our control.

5. Your images

By sharing an upscale, you allow Pixqo to display it in the app, and allow other users to view, like, save and share it. Pixqo doesn't claim ownership of images you create. AI-generated images may not be protected by copyright in every country.

6. Changes

If these rules change, we'll update this page and the date above. The privacy policy explains how data is handled.
